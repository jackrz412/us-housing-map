Where US mortgage risk is building

Mortgage defaults usually need two things at once: a household that is stretched (a heavy payment burden or thin equity) and a shock such as a job loss. These layers show where the first half is loaded, and where delinquency is already rising. They describe conditions; they don't predict defaults. How each layer is calculated.
