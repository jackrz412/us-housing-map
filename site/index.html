Where mortgage stress is building: a county-level view

Mortgage defaults usually need two things at once: a household that is stretched (a heavy payment burden or thin equity) and a shock such as a job loss. These layers show where the first half is loaded, and where delinquency is already rising. They describe conditions; they don't predict defaults. How each layer is calculated.

Grey hatching means Data not available: the source doesn't publish that county, or its sample is too small (fewer than 30 loans or owner households). Missing data is excluded, not estimated.

Colors use fixed breaks, so a color means the same thing in every county. Two layers use two-sided scales with a grey middle: delinquency is compared with each county's own 2017–2019 average, and owner cost burden with the US share. Blue means below that midpoint, not "safe".
